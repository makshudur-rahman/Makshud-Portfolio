About Me

ETE Graduate · RUET

Makshudur Rahman

Makshudur Rahman was born on 7 December 2002 in Mymensingh, Bangladesh, where he grew up. He is an Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering graduate from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh, having completed his undergraduate degree with a CGPA of 3.73 out of 4.00.

His academic foundation spans communication engineering, power electronics, control systems, and computational methods. His primary research focus is grid-forming inverter control and power-system stability, with additional interests in renewable-energy integration, intelligent control, metasurfaces, nanophotonics, plasmonic absorbers, and optical sensing.

His research experience includes studies on disturbance-adaptive control strategies for grid-forming inverters and polarization-insensitive metasurface absorbers. He is experienced in MATLAB/Simulink, programming, mathematical modeling, and engineering simulation, and enjoys applying these skills to challenging research and practical engineering problems.

Driven by intellectual curiosity and a commitment to professional growth, Makshudur aspires to pursue impactful research, collaborate across disciplines, and contribute to innovative engineering solutions that address real-world challenges.
